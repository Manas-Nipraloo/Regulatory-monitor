Regulatory Monitor - Session Summary
Date: 06-07-2026

Project Goal
- Build a Python/FastAPI regulatory monitor app.
- Daily workflow:
  1. Visit configured regulatory websites.
  2. Find current-date articles/documents.
  3. Download/process PDF documents.
  4. Upload only PDFs to Google Drive in date/remark folders.
  5. Extract headings and summaries.
  6. Draft an email in the required format.
  7. If no articles are found, do not upload anything and do not create an email draft.

- FastAPI app created and running on: http://127.0.0.1:8000
- Main endpoint: POST /tasks/daily-run
- Health endpoint: GET /health
